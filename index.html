About NCC

⚔️

What is NCC?

The National Cadet Corps is the largest uniformed youth organization in the world. Established in 1948 under the Ministry of Defence, it trains over 13 lakh cadets across Army, Navy and Air Wings annually.

🎯

Mission

To develop character, comradeship, discipline, leadership, secular outlook, spirit of adventure and ideals of selfless service — moulding young citizens into ideal future leaders of the nation.

🌐

Motto

"Unity and Discipline"

The NCC flag bears Red for Army, Deep Blue for Navy and Sky Blue for Air Force — unity across all three wings of the Armed Forces.

📋

Organisation

Headed by DG NCC, a Lt. General rank officer. 17 Directorates across India, 826 NCC units, training youth in all three wings of the Indian Armed Forces.

🎍

Certificates A · B · C

'A' for Junior Division. 'B' after 2 years Senior Division. 'C' — the highest NCC certificate. C-cert holders get special weightage in Armed Forces and Civil Services recruitment.

✈️

Air Wing

Trains cadets in aeronautics, gliding, microlight flying, ATC and ground subjects. Air Wing cadets can earn Flying Scholarships and march at the Republic Day Parade, New Delhi.

🪖

ARMY WING

Red colour on the NCC flag. Foot drill, field craft, obstacle courses, map reading and weapon training.

⚓

NAVY WING

Deep Blue on the flag. Seamanship, water sports, INS attachment camps and maritime awareness.

✈️

AIR WING

Sky Blue on the flag. Aeronautics, gliding, microlight flying, ATC training and Air Force attachments.
